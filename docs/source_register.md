# Source register

Every dataset below was downloaded on **22 October 2026** by `src/fetch_public_data.py`. It returned HTTP 200, and its byte size and SHA-256 are in `data/raw/manifest.csv`. I verified the fields and periods by parsing the files (`src/clean_public_data.py`, `src/parse_external_files.py`); the publishers' descriptions were not taken on trust.

Licences are as stated on each publisher's copyright page when checked on 22 Oct 2026. Re-confirm them before any commercial reuse.

## D. Recommended source combination and how the sources reconcile

**Core combination:** JSA Employment Projections (demand growth by occupation) + ABS EQ08 and Labour Force (WA supply) + JSA IVI and OSL (shortage and recruitment competition) + ABS Labour Account / JV (market tightness) + ABS WPI (wage growth) + ABS Census G54 (age profile) + ABS Job Mobility (turnover benchmark). The rest of the case study is internal, and here it is synthetic.

| Difference | Problem | How this project reconciles it |
|---|---|---|
| **Occupation classification** | EQ08 uses ANZSCO 2013; IVI, OSL and projections use ANZSCO 2022; JSA IVI 2-digit uses JSA's own groups | Join at 4-digit unit group. The codes used here (2544, 2541, 4114, 4231, 4233, 252x, 253x) are unchanged between 2013 and 2022. Fictional job families map to unit groups in `src/common.py` (`JOB_FAMILIES`); Admin and Support map to whole major groups 5 and 8 |
| **Industry classification** | ANZSIC 2006 division Q (Health Care & Social Assistance) includes social assistance and NDIS services, not just hospitals | Division-level series are context and backtest data only. Hospital-specific demand comes from occupation projections, not HCSA growth (HCSA grew 5.6% p.a., far faster than RN projections of about 2.4%) |
| **Geography** | API data is national or state; Census is GCCSA; IVI is state; internal sites are towns | Sites map to GCCSA (Greater Perth / Rest of WA) and SA4 in `LOCATIONS`. State indicators apply to every site; the regional distinction uses OSL "R" ratings and the internal time-to-fill and turnover data |
| **Time** | Monthly (LF, IVI), quarterly (JV, LA, WPI, EQ08), biannual (AWE), annual (Job Mobility, Feb), 2021 one-off (Census), financial year (internal) | Every series gets a `period_start` date and an Australian financial-year label (`fy_label`). Quarterly-to-FY conversion uses 4-quarter means. WA JV (original only) uses a 4-quarter rolling mean to remove seasonality |
| **Concept: vacancy** | ABS JV = unfilled jobs available now (employer survey); IVI = new online ads (a flow); internal vacancy = budgeted FTE − filled FTE | They are never added together. Each is used for what it measures: JV for the V/U ratio, IVI for relative competition per occupation, establishment gaps for internal vacancy rate |
| **Concept: turnover** | ABS Job Mobility = people leaving employment or changing employer across a whole industry; internal turnover = separations from one employer / average headcount | The Job Mobility figure (10.8%) is labelled a **benchmark**, not a target. One employer's turnover is expected to be higher because it includes moves to other health employers |
| **Seasonal adjustment** | Some series SA, some original only | SA used where published. Original series are smoothed or compared year-on-year. Mixed bases are never summed |
| **Totals** | Sub-series do not add exactly (perturbation, independent adjustment) | Tolerance-based reconciliation rules R1–R7 (see `data_quality_report.md`) |
