# Business case: Kestrel Health Services (fictional)

> **Kestrel Health Services (KHS) is a fictional organisation** created for this portfolio case study.
> All of its internal workforce data (employees, movements, requisitions, payroll, establishment, agency use) is **synthetic**, generated by `src/generate_synthetic_data.py`.
> External labour-market evidence is **real public data** (see `source_register.md`).

## Why a WA health provider

The sector choice follows the public evidence:

- **National shortage ratings.** The JSA Occupation Shortage List 2026 rates Registered Nurses (2544), Midwives (2541), all medical practitioner unit groups (2531–2539), Occupational Therapists and Physiotherapists as in shortage nationally. Enrolled Nurses (4114) are rated as a regional shortage. (`data/processed/osl_2026_unit_group.csv`)
- **Online demand.**
  - WA online job ads for Registered Nurses were 583.7 (3-month average) in September 2026, about **93% above the 2019 average**.
  - Medical practitioner ads were about **189% above** the 2019 average.
  - Source: JSA IVI, `outputs/tables/ext_occupation_indicators.csv`.
- **A tight WA market.** WA had **0.56 job vacancies per unemployed person** in the latest quarter, against a 2015–19 average of 0.24. WA unemployment was 4.2% in September 2026. (ABS JV and LF, `outputs/tables/ext_summary.json`)
- **An older workforce, especially outside Perth.** At the 2021 Census, **22.9%** of WA Health Care & Social Assistance workers were aged 55+, rising to **27.7%** in Rest of WA. (ABS Census G54)

## Executive question

> **How should KHS manage workforce shortages, vacancies, recruitment capacity and labour costs over FY2027–FY2029, and which actions should it fund first?**

## Business problem

KHS's agency spend has grown every year: from $3.6M in FY2023 to $6.6M in FY2026 (synthetic). The vacancy rate against establishment is 4.4% overall, but it is concentrated in regional sites (Broome 14.0%) and in clinical families. Time-to-fill for medical practitioners has a median of 101 days. Executives have no single, reconciled view of the workforce: HRIS, payroll and finance give different counts. They also have no forward view of where gaps will open.

## Planning horizon and scope

- **Horizon:** FY2027–FY2029 (1 Jul 2026 – 30 Jun 2029), annual steps.
- **History used:** FY2023–FY2026 movements; real external series back to 1994 for backtesting.
- **In scope:** employed workforce and agency/contractor use.
- **Out of scope:** volunteers, visiting medical officers on contract, and roster-level (shift) planning.

## Constraints

- No WA-specific shortage ratings exist (the OSL is national), so regional shortage is inferred from internal data and OSL "R" ratings.
- External data is at occupation or state level, not employer level, so internal turnover and time-to-fill cannot be benchmarked like-for-like.
- Budget: the establishment is set by Finance, and the FY2027 budget is 1,749.5 FTE, up from 1,701.8 FTE in FY2026 (synthetic).
- Recruitment capacity: demonstrated throughput is about 478 external hires a year across all cells.

## Success measures (proposed targets for executive agreement)

| Measure | Current (FY2026, synthetic) | Proposed target FY2029 |
|---|---|---|
| Vacancy rate (vacant FTE / establishment FTE) | 4.4% | < 3% overall; < 8% at any site |
| Turnover (separations / average headcount) | 14.5% | < 13% |
| Early attrition (left within 12 months of hire, FY2024–25 hires) | 24.2% | < 18% |
| Median time-to-fill (FY2026 fills) | 39 days | < 35 days; medical < 75 days |
| Agency FTE | 39.8 FTE ($6.6M) | Hold at or below FY2026 in real terms |
| Shortage FTE (model, FY2029) | n/a | < 30 FTE under Baseline assumptions |

## Key decisions the analysis supports

1. How many hires per year, by job family and site, and is recruitment capacity sufficient?
2. Where to invest first: retention, regional recruitment, grow-your-own pathways, or agency management.
3. What contingency to hold if the Constraint scenario materialises.
4. Which data improvements are needed before the model is used for budgeting.
